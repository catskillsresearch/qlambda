Typed linear qlambda

The active Lean development formalizes a Church-style linear/nonlinear quantum lambda calculus. The judgment Γ ; Δ ⊢ M : A separates reusable classical assumptions from exactly-once linear resources. Probability appears only at measurement.

Semantic layers

  • Quantum sets and operator-subspace relations with complete hom orders.
  • Tensor, internal hom, compact closure, and a concrete Set ⊣ qRel LNL model.
  • A separate enriched category of quantum CPOs and Scott-continuous quantum functions.
  • Projection-chain bilimits with inverse continuous shift maps.
  • Finite Kraus/CP presentations for gates, reset, and measurement.

Circuit correspondence

Deterministic staging produces a resource-certified circuit normal form. Every command in the declared two-qubit/two-bit quotation fragment has a canonical well-typed lambda representative. Compilation returns the original command and preserves ideal classical–quantum denotation.

Interchange boundary

Composer/OpenQASM is a frozen ideal interchange target, not the semantic model. Theorems cover canonical structured text and finite supported circuits; they do not cover arbitrary Qiskit programs, calibration, transpiler behavior, noise, or exact finite synthesis of every unitary.
